## Mary Lister McCammon Summer Research Fellowship
**Topic:** Hierarchical Spatial Bayesian Modelling of Social Contact Matrices <br>
**Institution:** Imperial College London, Department of Mathematics (MLGH) <br>
**Dates:** July - September 2025

*   [📄 View the Poster (PDF)](https://github.com/ljhason/academic-research-portfolio/blob/main/MLM_social_contact_matrices_poster.pdf)
*   **Summary:** Applied Bayesian Modelling, to infer (MCMC) coarse social contact intensity matrices. Using a second-order
Gaussian Markov Random Field prior, the model (Coarse-BRC) outperformed socialmixr on RMSE, MAE,
and 95% coverage metrics across smaller sample sizes. Applied Coarse-BRC to cleaned and preprocessed German census data and COVIMOD - a German, longi-
tudinal, COVID-era social contact survey which across five waves includes over 7,500 participants.
*   **Key Skills:** Bayesian Inference, GMRF, Spatial Modelling, Computational Statistics.

## Google DeepMind Research Ready Programme
**Topic:** Machine Learning and AI for SARS-CoV-2 MPro Inhibitor Discovery <br>
**Institution:** University of Oxford, Department of Statistics (OPIG) <br>
**Dates:** July - August 2025 <br>

*   [📄 View the Poster (PDF)](https://github.com/ljhason/academic-research-portfolio/blob/main/Deepmind_virtual_screening_poster.pdf)
*   **Summary:** Created and improved RF, SVM, and ANN regression models using Scikit-learn and COVID moonshot project data for protein-ligand binding affinity prediction (virtual screening of ligands). Applied statistical resampling techniques (k-fold cross-validation), performance metrics (MSE, MAE, R2),
and interpretability tools (SHAP) to evaluate and fine-tune both regression and classification RF models. 
*   **Key Skills:** Random Forests, SHAP, Optuna, RDKit, Scikit-Learn, Cheminformatics.
